# Micro-SaaS: Solo Developer Income Product
*With realistic expectations — no hype*

---

## Reality Check First

Micro-SaaS gets a lot of hype, but the honest numbers are: **in an analysis of 1,000+ micro-SaaS businesses, 70% generate under $1,000/month, and only 1-2% exceed $50,000/month.**

That doesn't mean don't try — it means **set realistic expectations** and don't base your decision on "overnight success" stories.

---

## PART 1 — WHAT IS MICRO-SAAS

```
Traditional SaaS   — big team, funding, broad market, complex product
Micro-SaaS         — solo/small team, self-funded, narrow niche,
                     simple focused product
```

The goal of micro-SaaS is **$2K-10K/month in side/full income** — not building a unicorn.

---

## PART 2 — HOW TO FIND AN IDEA

```
✓ Solve your own problem (one you faced at work)
✓ Build a "simpler, cheaper version" of an existing tool
   (big SaaS products get overcomplicated — niche
   simplification is where the money is)
✓ Choose a specific audience (e.g., "invoicing for
  freelance photographers" not "invoicing tool")
✓ Look for complaints on Reddit/niche communities
  about your target audience
```

**Green flag:** people are already doing this work some way (spreadsheet, manual process) and are frustrated by it.

---

## PART 3 — BUILD APPROACH (For a Solo Developer)

```
1. Keep the MVP scope buildable in 2-3 weeks
   (feature creep is the biggest killer of solo projects)
2. Use a stack you already know
   (learning new tech during the MVP phase wastes time)
3. Integrate payments (Stripe) and auth from day 1
   (retrofitting them later is painful)
4. Launch on Product Hunt / niche communities
   (get organic validation before paid ads)
```

### Realistic Timeline

```
Week 1-3:   MVP build
Week 4:     Launch (Product Hunt, relevant subreddit, X/LinkedIn)
Month 2-3:  Iterate on feedback, get first 10-20 paying customers
Month 4-6:  Decide: scale or pivot/sunset
```

---

## PART 4 — PRICING (Micro-SaaS Specific)

```
✗ Don't keep a free tier unless necessary
   (free users cost support, not revenue)
✓ Simple 2-3 tier pricing (something like $9, $29, $79/month)
✓ Offer an annual discount (for upfront cashflow)
✓ Ask your first customers directly what's missing
   (they have the best insight)
```

---

## PART 5 — HONEST TRADE-OFFS

```
Pro: Side income alongside your job, less client dependency
Pro: Your own product — becomes an equity-like asset
Pro: You learn skills (marketing, sales, support) a job
     doesn't teach you

Con: 70% of projects earn under $1K/month — many attempts
     fail, or stay at "lifestyle income" level
Con: Support/maintenance is an ongoing commitment — "build once,
     forget" doesn't work
Con: You need to learn marketing/sales — coding alone isn't enough
```

---

## PART 6 — CHECKLIST (Before You Start)

```
✓ You've identified a specific, narrow problem (not a broad tool)
✓ MVP scope is deliverable in 2-3 weeks
✓ Payment integration is planned from day 1
✓ You've decided on a launch platform (Product Hunt/community)
✓ You've set realistic expectations ($1-5K/month, not a unicorn)
```
