## CHUNK 1 — Introduction, Three Milestones & Why This Works

---

### The Core Promise

You don't need months to learn AI. The Getting Started section gives you a structured path to become productive fast.

**Three Milestones:**

| Time | What You Achieve |
|------|-----------------|
| ~6 hours | Productive with AI (Foundations + first Mode course) |
| 1 weekend | Ship your first Digital FTE (Courses 7–9) |
| 1 month | Fluent across the full Agent Factory stack (all 16 courses + cloud deployment) |

---

### Why This Works — The 3-Step Approach

Instead of trying to learn everything upfront, the method is:

1. **Overview first** — get the big picture
2. **Identify critical topics** — what actually matters
3. **Cover the essential 80%** — learn the rest through doing

> "The crash courses in this section get you working fast. The chapters in the book are your post-course reference — written to be returned to whenever a gap shows up in real work."

Traditional approaches fail because they try to be comprehensive upfront. This approach gets you working, then fills gaps as they appear.

## CHUNK 2 — Three-Layer Mental Model & Your Journey

---

### Start with the Thesis

The thesis is the foundation. After reading it, you have a three-layer mental model for all AI work:

```
Layer 1: Use general agents for problem-solving
           ↓
Layer 2: Build specialized AI Workers for specific jobs
           ↓
Layer 3: Assemble Workers into AI-Native Companies
```

**Important naming clarification:**
> "Throughout this book, AI Worker, Digital FTE, and AI Employee refer to the same idea — a specialized agentic system doing a real job under human-defined policy."

These three terms are interchangeable — they all mean the same thing.

## CHUNK 3 — Mode 1 vs Mode 2 & Starter Paths

---

### Two Modes from the Thesis

The thesis defined two operating modes. The Getting Started section maps these to concrete courses.

**Mode 1 — Problem-Solving**
- Using AI to accelerate YOUR work
- Tools: Claude Code / OpenCode, Claude Cowork / OpenWork
- Suitable for: anyone, no engineering background needed
- Starts at: Course 3 or 4

**Mode 2 — Manufacturing**
- Building AI Workers that operate independently
- Requires engineering expertise
- Key insight: "The general agent's output is not the outcome — it is the Worker that produces the outcome."
- Starts at: Course 7

---

### Mode 1 vs Mode 2 Comparison Table

| Factor | Mode 1 (Problem-Solving) | Mode 2 (Manufacturing) |
|--------|--------------------------|----------------------|
| Who | Anyone | Engineers |
| Goal | AI helps you work faster | AI Workers work independently |
| Tool type | General-purpose agents | Agents that build Workers |
| Starting course | Course 3 or 4 | Course 7 |
| Output | Completed work | Independent AI Workers |
| Framework | Seven Principles | Seven Invariants |
